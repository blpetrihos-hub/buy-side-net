Where the PRC price is the lower bid

Matched buy-side prices from the 30 September pitches. A gap is a measurement. The page does not recommend a response.

What this measures

This site is a net assessment of matched buy-side prices: what a buyer pays on a U.S. (or, when flagged, allied) offer versus what that buyer pays on a PRC offer for the same specification. A lower matched PRC buy-side price is the asymmetry. The page does not recommend a policy, publish a threat index, or blend a goods gap with a finance gap.

The clock is 2026–2036. Price observations may be dated 2021–2026 when that is the award or quote year. Mandate: U.S. and PRC capabilities in Latin America and, for the chip and finance lanes, the markets those pitches already named.

Four first baskets

  • commanding_heights / power_equipment — Latin American awards for transformers, turbines, HVDC, or inverters.
  • niobium / fenb_std — standard-grade ferroniobium, USD per kg.
  • ai_chips / usd_per_chip — Nvidia export SKU versus Huawei Ascend SKU, USD per chip, one calendar year.
  • icbc_finance / all_in_rate — all-in interest rate or spread; tenor within two years; public terms only.

Match rule

A row scores when all of these hold:

  • evidence is paired
  • both USD prices are present
  • same spec_class, same price_year, same buyer (or named market), same unit
  • us_side is us
  • FX is 1 because the quote was USD, or fx_usd and fx_date cite a public rate on the price date

Formula after USD conversion: gap = (us_price_usd − prc_price_usd) / us_price_usd. Positive gap means the PRC offer is cheaper for the buyer. The build script recomputes on_scoreboard; a hand-typed yes cannot put a bad row on the median.

Allied pairs (us_side=allied) can appear on the map but do not set the U.S. median. CBMM versus a PRC ferroniobium offer is allied until a U.S. seller is in the pair.

FX rule

Prefer quotes already in USD. Otherwise convert with a public rate on the price date (Fed H.10, IMF, or a named central bank) and store both fx_usd and fx_date. Never invent a rate.

What stays off the median

  • Proxy, one-sided, hunt, and exclude rows
  • Press-only figures marked UNVERIFIED
  • Pitch-deck color (market share, Ascend revenue projections, loan sizes without a rate)
  • Allied pairs
  • Finance gaps on the goods board, and goods gaps on the finance board
  • Any leaked ICBC internal record set — finance uses published articles and public loan terms only

Evidence classes

  • paired — both prices, match rules hold
  • one_sided — one price; off the median
  • proxy — list price, mismatched spec or year, revenue, market share, loan size, or press number not yet confirmed
  • hunt — named search with no price yet
  • exclude — not a buy-side price

Geography

Pin the project site when the source names it. Otherwise pin the buyer HQ or the seller HQ and say which in geo_note. No coordinates for a project the source does not locate. Those rows stay on the lists without a pin.

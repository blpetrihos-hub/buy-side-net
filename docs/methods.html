Commanding Heights of Latin America

PRC versus U.S. investment and presence across infrastructure, scarce natural resources, and energy.

What this measures

This site is a net assessment of the commanding heights of Latin America: comparative, diagnostic, and forward-looking. It compares U.S. and PRC investment and presence across three layers — Infrastructure, Scarce Natural Resources, and Energy — with allied/other non-PRC actors shown separately. The product is an asymmetry you can check. Descriptive. No recommendations, no threat score, no blended cross-layer index, no sentence that says the U.S. should.

The clock is 2026–2036. Observation years may run 2021–2026 when that is the award, deal, or quote year. Citations are Chicago. If you have not opened the page, the row does not exist. Press-only figures are UNVERIFIED. Leave lat/lon empty rather than guess.

Geography

Latin America and the Caribbean only. Mapped and readout-eligible rows require country in latin_america_caribbean. Out-of-region countries are forced to status=archived (layer may become archived if not already) and are never placed on the map. Hunt seeds may leave country blank until a named site exists; blank-country rows are not mapped. Excludes U.S. territories: Puerto Rico, USVI (archive us_territory_out_of_scope).

Allowed countries (codebook): Argentina, Belize, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Cuba, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Guyana, Haiti, Honduras, Jamaica, Mexico, Nicaragua, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay, Venezuela, Antigua and Barbuda, Bahamas, Barbados, Dominica, Grenada, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Trinidad and Tobago.

Evidence classes

  • paired — Both sides of a matched buy-side price (or comparable disclosed values) for the same specification and year. (on map; in USD sums; gap readout)
  • one_sided — One side’s disclosed value only. (on map; in USD sums)
  • proxy — Imperfect match, trade aggregate, market assessment, or press figure not yet confirmed by a primary document. Press-only figures are also marked UNVERIFIED in the note. (on map)
  • hunt — Named search with no priced observation yet. (on map)
  • exclude — Not an investment/presence observation for the net (context only). (off map and sums)
  • documented — Primary document (award, filing, parliamentary schedule) for a one-side observation. (on map; in USD sums)

FX rule

Prefer quotes already in USD (fx_usd=1). Otherwise convert with a public rate on the price date and store both fx_usd and fx_date. Never invent a rate.

What counts toward each readout

  • layer_counts — Per layer: count of active (non-archived, non-exclude) observations by side (us vs prc), with n.
  • layer_usd — Per layer: sum of disclosed value_usd by side, each with n. Only evidence classes with on_usd_sum true; proxy and hunt excluded from sums.
  • country_counts — Per country: count of observations by side (us vs prc), with n.
  • country_usd — Per country: sum of disclosed value_usd by side, each with n.
  • matched_gap — Secondary. Where evidence=paired and both value_usd and counterpart_value_usd exist: gap = (us_side_usd − prc_side_usd) / us_side_usd using the U.S. (or allied) figure as the denominator base when side is us/allied; otherwise gap = (counterpart_value_usd − value_usd) / counterpart_value_usd when the row’s side is prc and the counterpart is us/allied. Positive means the PRC figure is lower. Display with n. Never blend across layers.

Excluded from readouts:

  • archived status
  • exclude evidence
  • proxy value_usd (counts may show the row; USD sums do not)
  • hunt rows
  • empty value_usd

Bibliography

The public bibliography lists only sources cited (via observation source_id) by at least one on-map observation — status active or hunt on a non-archived layer (Infrastructure, Scarce Natural Resources, Energy). Sources cited only by archived rows, or by no row, are filtered out at render time; they remain in sources/bibliography.yml. The page is split into the three codebook layers, with subcategory groups where clean. Chicago citations and annotations are unchanged.

Public-source limits

  • Use procurement portals, company filings, ministry and utility notices, port-authority releases, published journalism.
  • Search in Spanish and Portuguese as well as English.
  • No paywalls or login walls; do not create accounts or bypass restrictions.
  • No leaked records; no personal data (counterpart means an agency, utility, firm, or named market).
  • One source family per hunt cycle; one commit per cycle.
  • Never invent a value, a coordinate, an actor, or a citation.

Smith’s rules (always in force)

  1. Method is net assessment: comparative, diagnostic, forward-looking. The product is an asymmetry you can check.
  2. Timeframe on this page is 2026–2036. Observations may be dated 2021–2026 when that is the award or deal year.
  3. Mandate: U.S. and PRC capabilities and presence in Latin America across the three layers.
  4. Voice: descriptive. The pages do not say “the U.S. should,” and they do not offer an implementation roadmap.
  5. Citations: Chicago. Never fabricate a source, a value, a buyer, or a coordinate.
